Does NEAR's fee switch pay for the chain?

The answer
The mechanism is real, but small. For every 10 new NEAR printed to pay validators, the fee switch buys back about 1.
To pay for itself at today's 2.5% issuance, NEAR Intents would need about $718M of swaps a day, 5.5x what it does now. The issuance cut passed on 2 November (HSP-029) lowers that bar to $459M a day once fully phased in (about 2029).

Three things to know

It is real

100% of payouts traced
Every USDT payout from the bridge's Tron wallet in one week ($97.0M) matches a public NEAR withdrawal. The money is not invented.

It is small

10.6% of new supply
Buybacks absorb about a tenth of the NEAR printed. The rest is new supply, so each holder's share of NEAR still shrinks.

The bar is coming down

$718M → $459M a day
Holders voted to cut issuance (HSP-029). Once phased in, the volume needed to pay for the chain falls by a third.

What could break it

All risks →
7 risks, ranked. Two are serious today; all are checked against the data on every rebuild.
We keep the bad news in. A thesis that hides its weak points can't be trusted on its strong ones.
High risk Bought-back NEAR is not retired
Buybacks page →

What we see

The 2.15M NEAR bought so far sits liquid in a multisig wallet (buybacks.multisignature.near). None of it is burned, staked or locked (0 NEAR locked; ledger C02 is FALSE).

Why it matters

A buyback only shrinks supply if the tokens stay out of circulation. Today the signers could sell or spend them, so our coverage figure is the best case, not a guarantee.

What would ease it

An on-chain burn, or a lock that only a holder vote can undo.
High risk Swap volume is falling
Fees page →

What we see

30-day volume is $3.9B, 19% below the 30 days before. DefiLlama's monthly change reads -41%.

Why it matters

Every dollar of buyback starts as a swap. Breakeven already needs 5.5x today's volume, and the gap widens when traffic drops.

What would ease it

Volume back above its September level, and a path toward $459M a day once HSP-029 is phased in.
Medium risk Buybacks are a choice, not a rule
Buybacks page →

What we see

Only 64% of NEAR's cut reached the buyback wallet in the last 30 days. The rest stayed in other revenue wallets.

Why it matters

No contract forces revenue into buybacks. The people running the wallets decide how much goes in and when, so the rate can drop without notice.

What would ease it

A published, on-chain sweep rule (for example: all revenue swept weekly).
Medium risk A few market makers fill most swaps
Volume quality page →

What we see

The largest solver fills 42% of sampled swap value; the top five fill 83%.

Why it matters

If one large market maker steps back, prices on Intents get worse, users leave and fees fall. The toll road depends on a handful of operators.

What would ease it

The top solver's share falling below a third as new solvers join.
Medium risk Fewer developers are building
Developers page →

What we see

582 monthly-active developers, down 28% in a year and 60% from the 2022 peak of 1,804. Full-time developers are up 6% to 278.

Why it matters

New apps bring new swap traffic. NEAR ranks 9 of 13 peer chains on active developers, so the pipeline of future fee payers is thin.

What would ease it

Two quarters of growth in monthly-active developers, not just full-time ones.
Medium risk Private volume cannot be checked
Volume quality page →

What we see

NEAR reports part of its volume as 'confidential'. Its share read 41% on 2026-10-31 and 0% on 2026-11-07, on the same total.

Why it matters

Nobody outside can see that private flow. Our custody check matched 100% of one week of USDT payouts on Tron to public withdrawals, but other routes are not reconciled.

What would ease it

NEAR publishing a verifiable total for confidential volume, or more routes passing our custody check.
Low risk The issuance cut could slip
Predictions page →

What we see

Holders passed HSP-029 on 2 November. It sits in a timelock until 16 November and then phases in over about two years.

Why it matters

Until it is live, breakeven stays at $718M a day. A delay or reversal keeps the bar at today's level.

What would ease it

The protocol's inflation setting changing on chain (our prediction P1 checks this).

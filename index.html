The mechanism is real and small. In the last 30 days NEAR Intents moved $3.9B, earned $6.3M in fees, and NEAR captured $2.1M of it (34%). 282,241 NEAR was bought against 2.65M issued: coverage 10.6%. Breakeven needs $718M/day of volume at 2.5% issuance ($459M/day at 1.6%), against $130M/day today.

What is going right: Intents is the largest venue of its kind DefiLlama can see (rank 1 of 30 bridge aggregators) and capture is rising. What is going wrong: issuance outruns buybacks 9x, bought NEAR is held not retired, base-layer activity has fallen, and Electric Capital counts 582 active developers, down 28% in a year. Both sides are in the ledger.

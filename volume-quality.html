Q7: repeat use

Of the destinations paid in the 2026-09 -> 2026-10 later month's sample, 2.6% were also paid in the earlier month's sample, carrying 34.6% of its value. With 1.16% of time sampled, both are lower bounds: a repeat user is only seen if both visits land in sampled minutes. Value-weighted repeat is the more informative figure because large repeat users are the most likely to be caught twice.

Q8: self-trade heuristic

On token_diff legs (direct swaps, 57% of signed intents), 5.0% of signer-pages show the same signer swapping A to B and B back to A inside one ~3-minute page (827 of 16516). Excluding the 38 persistent signers seen in 20 or more random pages (market makers), the rate is 0.8%. The 1Click flow hides the origin asset per user on-chain, so round-tripping there needs the Explorer feed.

Q10b: confidential payouts or coverage gaps? A custody check

A residual cannot separate private flow from missed data, so we reconcile custody instead. Confidential swaps hide the origin and route but still pay out on the destination chain as ordinary transfers (Aurora docs). For USDT on Tron we read every USDT transfer out of the bridge's Tron hot wallet (TX5XiRXdyz7sdFwF5mnhT1QoGCpbkncpke, TronGrid) and every WITHDRAW_TO receipt on the bridged-USDT contract (NearBlocks), 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-30, and matched them one to one: same recipient, amount less a bridge fee, sent within 3 hours. 100.0% of 13,749 payouts ($97.0M) are backed by a public withdrawal (median fee 0.86 USDT, median delay 12 s). 0.0% ($0, 0 payouts) left custody with no public record: that is the ceiling on unseen confidential payouts from this wallet. So a confidential payout on this route either passes through a public NEAR withdrawal (origin hidden, withdrawal visible) or leaves from a separate wallet we have not identified. 2 of 13,527 public withdrawals ($5.1k) had no payout from this wallet: the coverage gap on our side. A confidential swap whose output stays inside Intents never reaches custody and is invisible to any method.

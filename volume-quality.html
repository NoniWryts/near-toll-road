Q6: concentration

70% of payout value is signed by one-time 64-hex accounts that 1Click creates per quote, so counting signers counts quotes. That is why the Explorer reports 426,779 "unique users" for 426,878 swaps. Q7: repeat use

Of the destinations paid in the 2026-09 -> 2026-10 later month's sample, 2.5% were also paid in the earlier month's sample, carrying 36.3% of its value. With 1.17% of time sampled, both are lower bounds: a repeat user is only seen if both visits land in sampled minutes. Value-weighted repeat is the more informative figure because large repeat users are the most likely to be caught twice.

Q8: self-trade heuristic

On token_diff legs (direct swaps, 56% of signed intents), 5.1% of signer-pages show the same signer swapping A to B and B back to A inside one ~3-minute page (823 of 16192). Excluding the 33 persistent signers seen in 20 or more random pages (market makers), the rate is 0.8%. The 1Click flow hides the origin asset per user on-chain, so round-tripping there needs the Explorer feed.

Q9: channels

The chain does not label the front-end. Two proxies: who submits the settlement transaction (72% by intents.near, the 1Click relayer), and nearstats' top affiliate fee recipients on the Explorer feed (a recent window it does not state). The largest is buybacks.multisignature.near, the fee recipient of near.com's own app, at 3.4x the next one; then the SwapKit wallets (Ledger, Trust, Tangem) and LI.FI. The fee kept per channel is not public. escrow.ai.near submits a further 28% of settlement transactions.
